一、什麼是音階WHAT IS A SCALE

音階,是一串按高低排列的音,音與音之間具有一定的音程關係。「音階」是個統稱:五聲音階是音階,七聲音階是音階,大調、小調也都是音階的一種。本教材主要使用七聲音階,先聚焦在全音與半音的關係。

A scale is a series of notes ordered from low to high, with a characteristic pattern of intervals between the notes. "Scale" is a general term: pentatonic scales, seven-note scales, major and minor are all scales. This course works mainly with seven-note scales and focuses on whole and half steps.

二、大調音階與小調音階MAJOR AND MINOR SCALES

七聲音階裡最常見的,是大調音階和小調音階。大調音階的全半音排列是「全、全、半、全、全、全、半」,常聽起來明亮;小調音階(自然小調)是「全、半、全、全、半、全、全」,常聽起來較暗、較內斂。小調音階還有不同的形式,例如和聲小音階與旋律小音階,本教材在「小調音階變化」的練習裡介紹。

Among seven-note scales, the most common are the major scale and the minor scale. The major scale runs whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half, and often sounds bright; the (natural) minor scale runs whole, half, whole, whole, half, whole, whole, and often sounds darker and more reserved. The minor scale also comes in other forms, such as the harmonic and melodic minor scales, which this course covers in "Minor Scale Changes".

三、用七聲音階認識七個教會調式THE SEVEN CHURCH MODES

拿 C 大調音階的七個音——C、D、E、F、G、A、B,也就是鋼琴的白鍵——把其中不同的音建立成主音中心,就得到七個教會調式。音完全一樣,中心不同,色彩就不同:把 C 建立成主音中心,聽到的是 C 大調;把 D 建立成主音中心,同樣的白鍵就成了 D Dorian。所以「調式」和「音階」並不互斥:每個教會調式,本身就是一條七聲音階。本教材約定:這裡說的「調式」,指的是下面這七個教會調式;和聲小音階、旋律小音階則另外放在小調音階的變化裡。

Take the seven notes of the C major scale - C, D, E, F, G, A, B, the white keys of the piano - and build a different note as the tonal center: you get the seven church modes. The notes are exactly the same; a different center gives a different color. Building C as the center sounds like C major; building D as the center, the very same white keys become D Dorian. So "mode" and "scale" are not opposites: each church mode is itself a seven-note scale. In this course, "mode" means the seven church modes below; the harmonic and melodic minor scales are covered separately under minor scale changes.
